# About OrderWise

## The Idea Behind OrderWise

Running an online business isn't always easy. Apart from making sales, sellers also have to deal with cancelled orders, returned products, failed deliveries, and damaged items. These problems can lead to losses, especially when they happen regularly.

I started thinking about how online sellers could keep better track of these losses, and that's where the idea for OrderWise came from.

## The Problem

Most sellers pay attention to how much they sell, but keeping track of how much they lose can be just as important.

For example, a seller might lose money through delivery fees on failed orders, returned products, or damaged goods. Without proper records, it becomes difficult to know exactly where the money is going.

## The Solution

OrderWise is my attempt to make this easier.

The application will allow sellers to record their orders, track expenses, and calculate losses without having to do everything manually.

It will also help them identify common problems and understand how these losses affect their businesses.

## Who Is It For?

OrderWise is being built with the following users in mind:

* Small business owners.
* Online vendors.
* E-commerce entrepreneurs.
* Jumia and other marketplace sellers.
* Independent online retailers.

## My Goal

I want to build a simple but useful tool that helps online sellers understand their losses and make better business decisions.

The bigger goal is to help sellers spend less time worrying about where their money went and more time improving their businesses.
